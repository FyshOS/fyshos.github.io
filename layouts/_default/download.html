Getting Started

From download to desktop in three steps

1

Write the ISO to a USB stick

Use a tool such as balenaEtcher or dd to flash the image onto a USB drive of 2 GB or larger. The images are hybrid, so they also work when burned to a DVD.

2

Boot from the drive

Restart your computer and choose the USB drive from the boot menu. You may need to disable Secure Boot in your firmware settings first.

3

Try it, then install

FyshOS runs live from the USB stick so you can explore the Tyde desktop before committing. When you are happy, run the installer from the desktop to put it on your disk.
