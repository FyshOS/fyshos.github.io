Tyde Desktop

A fresh take on the Linux desktop experience. Tyde is designed to be simple to use, easy to develop for, and beautiful to look at.

Tyde brings a fresh look at desktop environments, focusing on what matters: getting things done while enjoying the experience. Built entirely with the Fyne toolkit and the Go programming language, it's fast, reliable, and easy to extend.

As an Open Source project, Tyde welcomes contributions of all kinds. Whether you want to fix bugs, add features, or create beautiful themes, there's a place for you.
